Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile applications developed by BB Labs LLC, including FuelTracker and Family Fitness Tracker.

FuelTracker

Information We Collect

FuelTracker collects only the information you choose to enter into the app, including vehicle names and fuel fill-up details such as dates, trip miles, gallons purchased, and fuel prices.

The app uses anonymous authentication provided by Google Firebase. No usernames, passwords, email addresses, or personal identifiers are required.

How Data Is Used

Data is used solely to provide FuelTracker’s core functionality, including syncing fuel records across devices within a shared household.

Family Fitness Tracker

Information We Collect

Family Fitness Tracker collects information necessary to provide its core functionality, including:

  • Household membership and roles
  • Profile display names (such as nicknames)
  • Monthly distance goals
  • Workout entries (date, activity type, distance, and optional duration)

Adults may sign in using Sign in with Apple. The app does not require users to provide personal information beyond what is necessary for authentication.

Children may use the app through anonymous authentication and are not required to provide an email address.

How Data Is Used

Data is used only to:

  • Create and manage household groups
  • Track progress toward monthly goals
  • Display workout history and earned badges

Data Storage

Application data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication). Data is accessible only to authorized devices within the same household configuration.

Data Sharing

BB Labs does not sell, rent, or share user data with advertisers or third parties. We do not use tracking technologies for cross-app or cross-website tracking.

Data Retention

Data remains stored until you choose to delete it from the app or request account deletion.

Children’s Privacy

Our applications are designed to be family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information from children. Parents or guardians may contact us to request deletion of household data.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user data. However, no system can guarantee complete security.
